9. Accessibility (A11y)

WCAG POUR Principles

PrincipleKey Requirements
PerceivableAlt text, captions, sufficient contrast (4.5:1)
OperableKeyboard navigation, visible focus, no flashing
UnderstandableClear language, predictable behavior, error identification
RobustValid HTML, proper ARIA, works with assistive tech
